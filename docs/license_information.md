# License Information


The License Information page shows the status, type and usage of your Veeam ONE license. On this page, you can install, update, renew or remove the license and submit license usage reports.

To open the page, click Configuration at the top right corner of the Veeam ONE Web Client window and then click License Information. The License tab shows the license details and consumption summary. The Instances, Points or Sockets tab (depending on your license type) shows consumption broken down by workload type.

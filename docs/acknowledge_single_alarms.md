# Acknowledging Individual Alarms


You can acknowledge individual alarms in both Veeam ONE Client and the Veeam ONE Web Client Alarms Overview screen.

Acknowledging alarms in Veeam ONE Web Client

To acknowledge individual alarms in the Veeam ONE Web Client Alarms Overview:

1. Open Veeam ONE Web Client.

For details, see [Accessing Veeam ONE Components](access.md).

1. Open the Alarms Overview section.
2. In the alarm list at the bottom of the screen, click on your desired alarm.
3. On the alarm details window, click Acknowledge.
4. In the Acknowledge Alarm window, specify a comment or a reason for acknowledging the alarm.
5. Click OK.

Acknowledging alarms in Veeam ONE Client

To acknowledge individual alarms in Veeam ONE Client:

1. Open Veeam ONE Client.

For details, see [Accessing Veeam ONE Components](access.md).

1. At the bottom of the inventory pane, click the necessary view (Veeam Backup & Replication, Veeam Backup for Microsoft 365, Virtual Infrastructure, VMware Cloud Director, Business View).
2. In the inventory pane, select the necessary object.
3. In the information pane, open the Alarms tab.
4. In the list of alarms, select one or more alarms and do either of the following:

* Right-click the selection and choose Acknowledge.
* In the Actions pane, click Acknowledge.

Press and hold the [CTRL] or [SHIFT] key to select multiple alarms.

1. In the Acknowledge Alarm window, specify a comment or a reason for acknowledging the alarms.

The message you specify will appear in the Comment field of the alarm history details, and in the email notification on acknowledged alarms. For details, see [Viewing Alarm History](view_alarm_history.md) and [Notifications on Acknowledged Alarms](acknowledged_notifications.md).

1. Click OK.

# Step 3. Accept License Agreements


At the License Agreement step of the wizard, read and accept Veeam license agreement, licensing policy, 3rd party components and required software license agreements. You will not be able to continue installation until you accept license agreements.

To read the terms of the license agreements, click the individual links.
